How the Mosaic Heart works

Each team in the Jax Healing Hearts Race builds its own heart of 17 pieces, from arteries to valves. Every gift reveals a piece in full color, along with the donor’s name and message.

  1. Pick a team. Tap any team on the leaderboard below to open its heart.
  2. Explore its heart. Hover over or tap a colored piece to see who gave and what they said. Black pieces are still waiting for a donor.
  3. Give. Use the Donate button on a team’s heart. Once the team records your gift, your piece peels away for everyone to see.

Team captain? Create your team’s heart or sign in to manage it.
